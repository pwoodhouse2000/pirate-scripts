DON'T PANIC

An unofficial text adventure, with graphics, in the spirit of The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy. New writing, new puzzles, same towel.

It is Thursday. Your house is about to be demolished, and then, in an escalation that will feel personal, so is your planet. You will need a towel, a pint, a packet of peanuts, a fish, a cup of tea, and the single most depressed robot in the galaxy. Not necessarily in that order. Actually, in roughly that order.

This is a fan-made homage, written from scratch in 2026 and inspired by the 1984 Infocom text adventure and Douglas Adams' books. It is unofficial, non-commercial, and not affiliated with the Adams estate, Infocom, or anyone with a legal department. All prose and puzzles here are original.

Type commands like look, take towel, talk to ford, put towel on drain, ask marvin about missiles. Click underlined words for a menu. The game saves itself after every move; type save for a manual save, undo to take back a move, hint when stuck, and guide topic to consult the Guide. 42 points are available. Of course they are.
